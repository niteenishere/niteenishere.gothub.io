Indian Farmers Data Story (2017-2022)

"From Raw Data to Policy Insight: Visualizing the Indian Farm Crisis."

This project utilizes exploratory data analysis (EDA) to map the correlation between state-level debt waivers and regional agricultural distress. By benchmarking outliers like Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh, I aimed to quantify the 'symptoms' of a larger crisis, proving that data-driven visualization is essential for effective policy decision-making.
